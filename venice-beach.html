About Venice Beach

One of LA's most iconic coastal stops

The Venice Boardwalk (Ocean Front Walk) stretches over two miles along the Pacific, drawing millions of visitors a year for street performers, skaters, beach culture, and classic SoCal energy. Beyond the boardwalk you've got Muscle Beach, the Venice Canals, Abbot Kinney's shops & restaurants, and easy access to Marina Del Rey and Santa Monica — all minutes from LAXCC.

Top spots near Venice

Plan a full day, then swing by LAXCC

Venice Beach Boardwalk

The two-mile tourist strip — street vendors, performers, ocean views, shops, and food. The classic Venice experience.

Venice Skatepark

Beachfront skatepark at 1800 Ocean Front Walk — one of LA's most photographed spots, right on the sand.

Muscle Beach Venice

The legendary outdoor gym — bodybuilding rings, calisthenics bars, and decades of LA fitness culture in the open air.

Venice Canals

Pedestrian bridges, canal-side homes, and a quiet alternative to the busy boardwalk — built in 1905, still gorgeous.

Abbot Kinney Blvd

Boutiques, coffee, restaurants, galleries, and bars — Venice's most-loved walkable shopping street.

Venice Pier

Quieter southern end of the boardwalk — fishing, sunset views, and fewer crowds than the central beach.

Marina Del Rey

Five minutes south — waterfront dining, harbor walks, and the calmer coastal vibe right next door to LAXCC.

Santa Monica

Just north of Venice — the Pier, Third Street Promenade, and the classic LA beach tourist circuit.
