Questions

Ordering weed online in LA, answered

Can I actually buy weed online in Los Angeles?

You can browse and reserve online, but California requires the sale itself to finish in person or through a licensed delivery driver. At LAX Cannabis Club you build the order on our online menu, we set it aside, and you finish the purchase at the counter with your ID.

Do I need a medical card?

No. We serve recreational customers 21 and over with a valid government-issued photo ID — driver's license, state ID or passport. Out-of-state and international IDs are fine.

Does LAX Cannabis Club deliver?

No — we don't deliver. Every order is pickup only. You build the cart online and we hold it under your name at 8332 Lincoln Blvd until you come and collect it. There's parking on site, and the counter is open until 9:30 PM every day.

How long does a pickup order take?

Usually well under an hour from order to ready, and most people are in and out of the shop in a few minutes. If you're on a tight layover, call (310) 429-3789 and we'll tell you exactly where your order stands.

What are your hours?

9:00 AM to 9:30 PM, every single day — no split schedule and no early Sunday close. Orders placed after hours are waiting when we open.

Is the online menu the same as what's in the store?

Yes. The online shop and the in-store register run off one inventory, so stock and pricing match. Deals move fast though — check the daily deals page before you build your cart.
