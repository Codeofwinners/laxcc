Discover Manhattan Beach

One of LA's cleanest, most upscale coastal cities — known for the pier, The Strand, elite volleyball, and a downtown that feels like a small beach town inside a metropolis. More polished than Hermosa, more lively than El Segundo.

Landmark

Manhattan Beach Pier

The 900-foot pier at the end of MB Blvd — sweeping ocean views, year-round fishing, and the Roundhouse Aquarium at the end.

Iconic

The Strand

World-famous paved path along the sand — walk, bike, or skate past multi-million dollar beachfront homes from Manhattan to Hermosa.

Downtown

Downtown MB

Walkable, upscale, and packed with boutiques, award-winning restaurants, and coffee shops — cleaner than Venice, less touristy than Santa Monica.

Architecture

MB Library

A modern architectural gem with floor-to-ceiling ocean views — a quiet spot to recharge with a different angle on the South Bay coast.

Culture

Beach Volleyball

The home of pro beach volleyball — AVP courts year-round at the pier, plus the Manhattan Beach Open every August.

Nearby

El Porto & LAX

El Porto for the surf crowd just north, LAX a quick 8-minute drive — Manhattan Beach is the South Bay's polished gateway.
