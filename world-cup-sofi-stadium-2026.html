SoFi Stadium becomes "Los Angeles Stadium" for the World Cup

For the first time, the FIFA World Cup 26™ is co-hosted across the United States, Canada and Mexico — and Los Angeles is one of the headline cities. All of LA's matches are played at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, the same indoor-outdoor venue that hosted Super Bowl LVI and will open the 2028 Olympics.

One quirk worth knowing: during the tournament (June–July 2026), FIFA rules require host stadiums to drop their commercial naming rights. So on your ticket, on the signage and in FIFA's official communications, SoFi will be called "Los Angeles Stadium." Same building, same address — don't let the name throw you off.

The full tournament runs June 11 – July 19, 2026 across all 16 host cities, with the opening match in Mexico City and the final in New Jersey. Here in LA, SoFi hosts eight matches from June 12 to July 10: five group-stage games, two Round of 32 knockouts, and a quarter-final. It kicked off in style — the U.S. Men's National Team beat Paraguay 4–1 in their opener on Friday, June 12, with a Folarin Balogun brace and a Gio Reyna trivela (recap). The USA closed the SoFi group stage on June 25 with a 3–2 loss to Türkiye on a stoppage-time goal, but had already done enough to win Group D and advance to the knockouts. With the five group games complete, attention now turns to the two Round of 32 ties and the July 10 quarter-final.
