Visitor Guide

Explore Hawthorne: The Hub of the South Bay

Centrally located near LAX and SoFi Stadium, Hawthorne is a dynamic city with deep roots in aerospace and music history. While it has a practical, industrial-commuter vibe, it is home to world-changing companies and a growing cannabis culture.

SpaceX & Rockets

Hawthorne is the world headquarters of SpaceX. While there are no public tours, you can drive by the facility to see the massive Falcon 9 booster standing tall outside—a perfect photo-op for aerospace fans.

Beach Boys Landmark

Visit the Beach Boys Historic Landmark at 3701 W. 119th St. This monument marks the childhood home where the Wilson brothers lived and created the music that defined the California sound.

Craft Breweries

Hawthorne has a booming brewery scene. Stop by Common Space Brewery or LA Ale Works for fresh craft beer, food trucks, and a local vibe just minutes from the SpaceX facility and LAXCC.
