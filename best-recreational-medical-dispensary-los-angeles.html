Recreational & Medical, Answered

The quick facts on shopping recreational cannabis and medical marijuana at LAXCC.

What's the difference between recreational and medical at LAXCC? +
Recreational cannabis is for any adult 21+ with a valid photo ID — no card or paperwork needed. Medical marijuana is for patients 18+ who have a valid physician's recommendation; medical patients with a state MMIC card can also save on tax. Both shop the same store and much of the same menu.
Do I need a medical card to buy weed in Los Angeles? +
No. Recreational marijuana is fully legal for adults 21+ in California — just bring a valid government-issued ID. A medical recommendation is only required if you're 18–20 or want medical-specific benefits like MMIC tax savings.
What ID do I need to bring? +
A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport). Medical patients 18–20 should also bring their physician's recommendation, and an MMIC card if you have one.
Where is LAXCC and what are the hours? +
LAXCC is at 8332 Lincoln Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90045 — on Highway 1 between LAX and Santa Monica, about 5 minutes from the airport. Open daily, 9 AM–9:30 PM. Call (310) 429-3789.
Can I order online for pickup? +
Yes. Browse and reserve on the live menu, then pick up in store — the fastest way in and out. Walk-ins are always welcome too.
