About Culver City

The heart of West LA's entertainment industry — home to Sony Pictures, Amazon Studios, Apple, TikTok, and the historic Helms Bakery District. Premium dining, indie boutiques, and one of LA's most walkable downtowns — all minutes from LAXCC.

Studios

Sony Pictures

The historic MGM lot, now home to Sony Pictures Entertainment — Spider-Man, Jeopardy!, and decades of Hollywood history.

Tech & Media

Amazon, Apple, TikTok

Amazon Studios HQ, Apple's growing campus, and TikTok's LA office — Culver is the West Side's tech hub.

Downtown

Culver Steps

The new heart of downtown — restaurants, shops, public plaza, and the historic Culver Hotel right across the street.

District

Helms Bakery

Iconic mid-century complex now full of furniture showrooms, design studios, and standout restaurants like Father's Office.

Outdoors

Ballona Wetlands

Protected coastal wetlands minutes south — trails, birding, and a quiet escape from the studios.

Dining

Downtown Culver

One of LA's most walkable food scenes — Akasha, Versailles, Bossa Nova, Petit Trois, and a packed weekly farmers market.
