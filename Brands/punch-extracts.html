Know Your Product

Understanding Cannabis Concentrates

Concentrates pack more punch per hit than flower. Here's what you need to know.

1
Extraction Methods
Solventless (ice water, heat press) vs. hydrocarbon (BHO, PHO). Solventless is prized for purity; hydrocarbon excels at preserving certain profiles.
2
Live vs. Cured
Live resin is made from fresh-frozen flower, preserving peak terpenes. Cured resin uses dried flower — different flavor, still potent.
3
Potency
Concentrates range from 60-90%+ THC. A little goes a long way — start with a small dab if you're new.
4
Textures
Badder, sauce, diamonds, rosin, shatter — each has a different consistency and dabbing experience. Try a few to find your preference.
5
Lab Testing
Every batch tested for cannabinoid content, residual solvents, pesticides, and contaminants. Clean concentrates are non-negotiable.
