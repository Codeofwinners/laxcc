Cannabis Wellness Products Explained

Cannabis wellness products offer an alternative to smoking. Here's what to know.

1
Topicals
Creams, balms, and lotions applied directly to the skin. Most won't get you high — they deliver localized cannabinoid relief.
2
Tinctures
Liquid extracts taken under the tongue for fast absorption. Effects in 15-30 minutes. Easy to dose precisely with a dropper.

Patches
Transdermal patches deliver cannabinoids through the skin over several hours. Consistent, long-lasting, discreet.

Many wellness products come in CBD:THC ratios like 1:1 or 3:1. Higher CBD ratios offer relief with less psychoactive effect.
