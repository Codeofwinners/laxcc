Know Your Product

What to Know About Cannabis Edibles

Edibles hit different than smoking. Here's what every customer should know before buying.

1
Precise Dosing
Quality edibles deliver consistent mg per piece. Look for brands that test every batch for accurate dosing — no hot spots, no surprises.
2
Onset Time
Edibles take 30-90 minutes to kick in because they're processed through your digestive system. Don't redose too soon.
3
Duration
Effects typically last 4-8 hours — much longer than smoking or vaping. Plan accordingly, especially if you're new.
4
Start Low
If you're new to edibles, begin with 2.5-5mg THC and wait at least 2 hours before taking more. You can always eat more, never less.
5
Lab Testing
Every batch is tested for THC/CBD accuracy, pesticides, and contaminants. What's on the label is what's in the package.
