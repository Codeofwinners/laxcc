Know Your Flower

Why Indoor-Grown Weed Hits Different

Not all cannabis flower is grown the same. Here's what makes indoor cultivation — like Stereo's — worth the premium.

1
Controlled Environment
Light, temperature, humidity, and CO2 are dialed in at every stage. The plant gets exactly what it needs, nothing it doesn't.
2
Denser Trichomes
Indoor light intensity drives heavier trichome production — that frosty, sticky coating packed with THC and terpenes.
3
Louder Terpenes
No wind, sun, or rain degrading volatile terpenes. Indoor flower preserves the full aromatic profile from grow to bag.
4
Tighter Buds
Consistent light exposure produces dense, compact nugs instead of loose, airy flower. Better bag appeal and smoother burns.
5
Cleaner Product
No outdoor contaminants, no pesticide pressure from open-air grows. Indoor flower passes lab tests cleaner and more consistently.
