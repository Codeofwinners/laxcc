Know Your Product

What Makes Premium Cannabis Flower

Not all flower is created equal. Here's what separates top-shelf bud from everything else.

1
Trichome Density
More trichomes means more THC and terpenes. Premium flower is visibly frosty — that crystal coating is where the potency lives.
2
Terpene Profile
Flavor and effects come from terpenes, not just THC percentage. Quality flower has a loud, complex nose you can smell through the bag.
3
Cure & Trim
Proper curing preserves potency, smoothness, and flavor. A clean hand-trim with no excess leaf means a better burn.
4
Lab Testing
Every batch is tested for THC/CBD potency, pesticides, heavy metals, mold, and residual solvents. No guesswork.
5
Proper Storage
Airtight containers, cool temperatures, no direct light. Fresh flower smokes smoother and tastes better.
