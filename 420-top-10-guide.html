Rank #9 · Live Rosin

Drops

Solventless live rosin — the purest form of California cannabis

Drops makes live rosin — a solventless concentrate pressed from fresh-frozen flower using nothing but heat and pressure. No butane, no CO2, no chemicals ever touch the plant. Because the flower is flash-frozen right after harvest and never dried, the full terpene profile survives the extraction. That's why rosin tastes like the living plant itself — fruit, gas, citrus, flowers — instead of the flat "weed" taste of most concentrates.

Live Rosin Solventless Fresh-Frozen Flower Heat + Pressure Only Full-Terpene Lab Tested
Who it's for

Flavor chasers and dab heads who want to taste the terpenes, not cover them up. Live rosin is the top shelf of the concentrate world — premium, potent, and expensive for a reason. If you've only ever smoked flower and want to see what California can actually do, this is the tier to try.
