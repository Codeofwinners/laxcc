10 Places You Can Legally Smoke Weed Near Los Angeles in 2026

By LAX Cannabis Club

If you want the accurate version of this topic, start with the city line. Inside the City of Los Angeles, commercial cannabis smoking lounges are still prohibited. The City of Los Angeles cannabis local-law framework says consumption lounges are currently prohibited. So if you mean Hollywood, Venice, Downtown LA, Silver Lake, or other places inside LA city limits, this is not a list of legal public lounges there.

Near Los Angeles is different. Neighboring cities such as West Hollywood and Hawthorne do have publicly supportable lounge models. West Hollywood's municipal code defines cannabis consumption areas with on-site retail for smoking, vaping, and ingestion, and Hawthorne's cannabis approvals include lounge operators. At the state level, Business and Professions Code Section 26200(g) allows local jurisdictions to permit smoking, vaporizing, and ingesting on licensed retailer or microbusiness premises if specific conditions are met.

California's official consumer guidance says you can use cannabis on private property, but not in public places, and Health and Safety Code Section 11362.3 still blocks public smoking outside the Section 26200 framework. That means this list has two different categories only: legal lounges in nearby cities, and legal private-property options. It does not treat secluded beaches, hikes, parking lots, or other public spots as legal workarounds. If you only want public-facing businesses, skip to #8 through #1.

Executive Summary

  • Los Angeles city proper: No commercial smoking lounges under the current city framework.
  • Best legal lounge clusters nearby: West Hollywood first, Hawthorne second.
  • State law: Section 26200(g) allows local authorization for on-site smoking, but only under strict conditions.
  • Private property: Legal only when the owner allows it.
  • Public spots: Beaches, hikes, scenic overlooks, sidewalks, parking lots, and cars still do not make this list.

What Makes This List Defensible

Each public-facing lounge below has an active state retailer premises record plus public evidence that the venue operates in a city that allows smoking lounges. The two private-property entries stay on the list because they are legal routes for people who want a lower-key, away-from-the-crowd option without inventing fake loopholes around beaches, hikes, or public land.

10. A Private Patio or Backyard Where the Owner Says Yes

If you want something outdoor and low-key, a truly private residential patio, backyard, or similar private space with the property owner's permission is the cleanest legal answer. California's official guidance says cannabis can be used on private property, not in public places.

This does not override lease terms, HOA rules, condo rules, or nuisance complaints. Private means genuinely private. A shared courtyard, front stoop, apartment walkway, or "hidden" patch of sand does not count.

Evidence: California's official consumer guidance says cannabis can be used on private property, while Health and Safety Code Section 11362.3 is why beaches, hikes, sidewalks, and other public places still do not qualify.

9. A 420-Friendly Rental or Stay With Written Smoking Permission

The second-best low-profile option is private lodging that clearly allows cannabis smoking in writing. That might be a 420-friendly rental, a boutique stay with a designated smoking area, or a private host who specifically approves cannabis use on the property.

Bud and Breakfast's Los Angeles page is one public example showing that cannabis-friendly stays are actively marketed in the area. But that listing alone is not the legal rule. The legal rule is still private property plus host permission plus compliance with house rules.

Do not assume "420-friendly" automatically means flower is allowed. California law still lets property owners ban cannabis use, so the smart move is to get written confirmation about where smoking is allowed before you book.

Evidence: Bud and Breakfast Los Angeles listings plus California's official private-property guidance.

8. Catalyst Hawthorne

Catalyst Hawthorne has a credible public paper trail. Its state premises page shows active retailer license C10-0001427-LIC at 14115 Crenshaw Boulevard, and Hawthorne's official cannabis approval page lists Catalyst as approved for Retail, Distribution, Lounge, Delivery.

Hawthorne then made the lounge status even clearer on April 1, 2025, when the city described Catalyst as its second cannabis retail and lounge space. Catalyst's own site also markets a dedicated Hawthorne cannabis lounge, which is enough to put it on the board as a real legal option near LAX.

7. The Artist Tree Hawthorne

The Artist Tree Hawthorne is another legitimate South Bay lounge. Its state premises page shows active retailer license C10-0001469-LIC at 4756 W Imperial Highway, and Hawthorne's official cannabis approvals list shows The Artist Tree approved for Retail, Lounge, Delivery.

The City of Hawthorne then publicly celebrated the opening on August 20, 2024, describing the business as a dispensary, lounge, and art space. The Artist Tree's own lounge page also markets Hawthorne as a dedicated lounge near LAX, which makes this one of the clearest non-WeHo options in the region.

The cleanest public lounge description here comes from Emerald Village rather than a dedicated lounge microsite, so it does not outrank the heavyweights above it. Still, it sits inside the West Hollywood framework that explicitly authorizes smoking, vaping, and ingestion lounges.

Original Cannabis Cafe belongs on any serious LA-area list because the public claims are unusually direct. Its state premises page shows active retailer license C10-0000600-LIC in West Hollywood, and the cafe's own site says customers can "eat, drink and smoke cannabis" on its patio.

This is not just cannabis-adjacent hospitality. It is a real, smoke-friendly cafe in a city that already authorizes smoking consumption areas, which is exactly the combination you want if you are trying to stay on the right side of the law.

The Woods still takes the top spot because the public record and the customer-facing lounge model line up cleanly. If somebody asks for the single easiest lounge to defend from public sources, this is still the answer. The Woods is not just a retailer that happens to tolerate consumption; it is openly positioned as one of West Hollywood's flagship cannabis-lounge experiences.

How To Verify A Lounge Before You Go

  • Check the city first: Los Angeles city proper is still a no for commercial smoking lounges, so start with the jurisdiction before you believe the branding.
  • Check the exact address: Use the state-run Real CA Cannabis records to verify the premises and license status.
  • Confirm smoking, not just "consumption": Look for smoking-specific language, city consumption-area records, or direct lounge rules, not just a cool patio and weed-themed decor.
  • For private property, get permission in writing: The owner or host can still ban cannabis use even on private land.
  • Do not treat public places like loopholes: Beaches, hikes, sidewalks, scenic overlooks, parking lots, and cars still fail the legal test even when they feel secluded.
