Discover Playa del Rey

A laid-back coastal pocket tucked between Marina del Rey and LAX — quieter than Venice, grittier than Manhattan Beach. Where locals come for surf, sunsets on the dunes, and Dockweiler bonfire nights.

The beach

Dockweiler State Beach

Three-mile stretch of sand south of the jetty — the only LA County beach where bonfires are legal. Sunsets right under the LAX flight path.

Coastal route

Vista Del Mar

The scenic two-lane road hugging the dunes from PDR to Manhattan Beach — one of the best coastal drives in LA.

Wetlands

Ballona Wetlands

600 acres of protected coastal wetlands — trails, birding, and a rare quiet escape between LAX and Marina del Rey.

Local culture

PDR village

Small downtown around Culver and Pershing — Cantalini's, Tower 42, and a handful of bars that feel like the old LA beach town it used to be.

Bike path

The Strand

Pick up the LA bike path at Dockweiler and ride all the way to Torrance — 22 miles of uninterrupted coast.

What's the closest dispensary to Playa del Rey Beach?

LAX Cannabis Club at 8332 Lincoln Blvd is one of the closest licensed dispensaries to Playa del Rey — roughly 2.2 miles up Lincoln Blvd, a 5-minute drive from the coast.

How far is LAXCC from Dockweiler Beach?

From Dockweiler State Beach it's typically 5 minutes by car — even faster from the Vista Del Mar parking lots. Easiest stop on your way back from a bonfire or sunset.

Can I order online for pickup before I head to the beach?

Yes. Browse the menu at laxcc.com/shop, place your order, and we'll have it bagged and ready when you arrive. Most pickups are ready in 30 minutes or less.

Can I consume cannabis at Playa del Rey or Dockweiler Beach?

No. California prohibits cannabis consumption in any public space — including beaches, parks, sidewalks, and vehicles. Consumption is private property only. Edibles, tinctures, and patches are smoke-free options that keep you compliant for the trip back.

Do I need to be a California resident to shop?

No. California allows any adult 21+ (or 18+ with a valid medical cannabis card) to purchase. International and out-of-state IDs are accepted — passport, driver's license, or military.

Is parking easy at LAXCC?

Yes. Free parking on Lincoln Blvd right outside the shop. Order ahead online, pull up, grab your bag, and you're back on the road in minutes.
