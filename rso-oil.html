The Founders Story

A Modern Approach to Cannabinoid Education

Founded by Casey, a Stanford-trained oncology nurse, Emerald Bay Extracts was born from a direct observation in a clinical setting. Casey recognized that many individuals were seeking out full-spectrum options for their personal wellness routines, but often lacked access to clear, evidence-based guidance and consistent product standards.

"Patients needed clinical-grade cannabis products and real medical guidance. Emerald Bay was born to bridge that gap."

Today, Emerald Bay operates out of Nevada City, California, maintaining the same rigorous standards found in healthcare: precision dosing, strain-specific integrity, and unparalleled transparency.

What is RSO?

Rick Simpson Oil (RSO) is a foundational full-spectrum extract that preserves the complete profile of the cannabis plant. Unlike distillate, which isolates THC, RSO retains the essential cannabinoids, terpenes, and flavonoids that work together in the "Entourage Effect."
