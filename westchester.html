Tourist Guide

Best Things to Do Near Westchester

Westchester is the perfect base for exploring the coastal Westside and South Bay. Here are the best spots within a 10-minute drive of your neighborhood dispensary.

Dockweiler State Beach

The closest beach option with 3.7 miles of ocean frontage. Famous for its beachfront fire pits (first-come, first-served), it's the ultimate spot for sunset bonfires, biking, and volleyball.

Playa del Rey

A low-key, local alternative to Venice. Perfect for a quiet beach walk, kite flying, or grabbing food at local cafés. It offers a much calmer vibe while remaining close to the LAX hub.

Flight Path Museum

A must for aviation fans. Located right at LAX, it features commercial aviation history, vintage airline uniforms, and space exploration exhibits that detail the rich history of flight in Los Angeles.

Clutter’s Park (Plane Spotting)

Located in nearby El Segundo, this park offers sweeping runway views. It’s one of the best spots in the city to watch planes take off and land from comfortable benches.

Marina del Rey

Just minutes away, the Marina offers waterfront dining, yacht views, and a relaxing harbor atmosphere. It’s a great spot for a more traditional "waterfront getaway" experience.

LAX Theme Building

The futuristic, UFO-like icon of LAX. Completed in 1961, it’s a stunning landmark for photos and a masterpiece of mid-century modern architecture.

The Best Tourist Plan

Catch the sunset at Dockweiler or Playa del Rey, then head to Marina del Rey or Westchester for dinner. For aviation lovers, Clutter’s Park or the Flight Path Museum are essential stops on your way to or from LAXCC.
