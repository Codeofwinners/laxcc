Frequently asked

Can I buy Coldfire Extracts direct from the brand?

No. Coldfire doesn't sell direct-to-consumer or deliver. Their products are only available through licensed California dispensaries. LAXCC is one of them — shop the live Coldfire menu above for what's in stock today.

Does Coldfire do collab drops with other brands?

Yes. Coldfire works directly with cultivators on limited collab drops. Recent partners include Fig Farms, Lumpy's, Team Elite Genetics, Booney Acres, Green Dragon, Blem, and Flight Path. Collab batches are strain-specific and tend to sell out fast — order early when you see one in our menu.

What makes Coldfire different from other vape brands?

Three things: (1) single-pass extraction below -100°F to preserve terpenes, (2) no additives, no distillate, no cutting agents in any of their oil, and (3) cultivar-specific runs — each batch tastes like the strain it came from, not generic "OG flavor."

Live Resin vs. Cured Resin Juice Carts — what's the difference?

Live Resin is made from fresh-frozen flower — flash-frozen at harvest to lock in volatile terpenes that would otherwise dissipate during drying. Cured Resin uses traditionally dried & cured flower, so the terp profile is closer to what you'd smoke in a joint. Both are full-spectrum at Coldfire — pick by flavor preference.

Do I need a medical card to buy Coldfire?

No. California allows any adult 21+ (or 18+ with a valid medical card) to purchase. No residency required — out-of-state and international IDs are accepted.

What hardware works best with Coldfire concentrates?

Live Diamond Sauce and Resin Badders perform best on a quartz banger at 500–550°F (low-temp dabs) — preserves the single-pass terpenes you paid for. E-rigs (Puffco, Dr. Dabber) work great at similar temps. Sauce can also be added to a bowl of premium flower for a flavor boost.
