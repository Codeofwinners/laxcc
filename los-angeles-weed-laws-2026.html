01 — Eligibility

Is Weed Legal
in Los Angeles?

Yes. Cannabis is fully legal in Los Angeles for adults under California's Prop 64. Here are the weed laws in Los Angeles that matter most — recreational buyers just need a valid ID, and medical patients get a couple of extra perks.

badge

Recreational — 21 and Over

Any adult 21+ can buy recreational cannabis with a valid government-issued photo ID. No card, no paperwork, no residency requirement — visitors and tourists are welcome too.

medical_services

Medical — 18 and Over

Patients 18+ with a valid physician's recommendation can shop medical cannabis. A state MMIC card can also exempt qualifying purchases from sales & use tax.

LA Weed Law FAQs

Is weed legal in Los Angeles in 2026?add

Yes. Recreational cannabis is legal for adults 21+ across California, including Los Angeles, and medical cannabis is available to patients 18+ with a valid physician's recommendation. Buy only from state-licensed dispensaries.

How much weed can I buy in one day?add

Adult-use customers can buy up to 28.5 grams (1 ounce) of flower, up to 8 grams of concentrate (including edibles/vapes by THC content), and up to 6 immature plants per day. Medical patients may qualify for more with a valid recommendation.

Can I smoke on the beach or in public?add

No. Public consumption is illegal in California — that includes sidewalks, parks, beaches, the Santa Monica Pier, and inside vehicles. Consume only on private property where it's permitted, or at a licensed lounge.

Do I need a medical card to buy weed?add

No. Any adult 21+ can buy recreational cannabis with a valid photo ID. A medical recommendation is only needed if you're 18–20 or want medical-specific benefits like MMIC tax savings.

Can tourists buy weed in LA?add

Yes. There's no residency requirement — any visitor 21+ with a valid government ID can shop at a licensed LA dispensary. Just remember you can't consume in public or take cannabis across state lines.

Can you buy weed in California without a dispensary?add

Not legally. Every legal cannabis sale in California has to go through a state-licensed retailer — either in person at a dispensary or by licensed delivery to your door. Buying from anyone else (a "plug," a pop-up, or an unlicensed shop) is illegal, and that product is untested, so it can contain pesticides, mold, or mislabeled THC. Before you buy, confirm the seller's license through the DCC — a legal LA dispensary menu lists lab-tested products only.
