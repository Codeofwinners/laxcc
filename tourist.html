04 · The Fine Print

Where to actually smoke.

Buying is the easy part. Knowing where to consume — and where not to — keeps your LA trip drama-free.

Where you can

  • Private residences: Your hotel room, Airbnb, or private property — check property rules first.
  • Licensed consumption lounges: Specific venues licensed for on-site use.
  • Some designated areas: Check the local ordinance before lighting up.

Where you can't

  • Public spaces — parks, beaches, sidewalks, the boardwalk.
  • Inside any vehicle or while driving.
  • Most hotels — check the individual hotel's policy.
  • Federal property, including all airports.
  • Schools and anywhere minors are present.
  • Workplaces (unless specifically allowed).

Getting around

  • Never consume while driving or as a passenger.
  • Store products sealed in the trunk while in transit.
  • Take a rideshare if you plan to consume.
  • Public transit policies vary — check before you travel.
Heading to the airport

Don't bring it past TSA.

Cannabis cannot go through TSA security or across state lines — not even to states where it's legal. Consume or dispose of all products before you head to LAX.
