Silicon Beach Guide

The Playa Vista Lifestyle

Known as the heart of Silicon Beach, Playa Vista is LA's most modern, planned community. From the historic Hughes Aircraft hangar (now Google's campus) to the polished vibe of RUNWAY, it's a neighborhood defined by tech, innovation, and walkable luxury.

Historic Heritage

Playa Vista sits on ground that once housed the aviation empire of Howard Hughes. This is where the legendary Spruce Goose was built. Today, that legacy of innovation continues with tech giants like Google and Facebook calling this area home.

The Vibe

Unlike the gritty energy of other LA areas, Playa Vista is polished, green, and highly walkable. It's a mix of tech professionals, luxury condos, and beautifully maintained parks like Central Park and Ballona Discovery Park.

Top Things to Do

  • RUNWAY Playa Vista: The neighborhood's premier lifestyle center. Grab a bite at dan modern chinese, LOQUI, or SOL Mexican Cocina before your visit to LAXCC.
  • Cinemark Playa Vista: Catch a movie in luxury XD recliners—a perfect follow-up to a premium cannabis experience.
  • Ballona Wetlands: Explore over 600 acres of coastal nature. A serene, low-key walk perfect for nature lovers.

Cannabis in Playa Vista

Essential information for residents and Silicon Beach visitors.

Local Availability

Currently, there are no licensed dispensaries within the immediate Playa Vista boundaries. LAX Cannabis Club serves as the primary premium dispensary for the community, located just 3 miles south (approx. 7–10 minutes).

Possession Limits

Per California law, adults 21+ can possess up to 28.5 grams of flower or 8 grams of concentrate. Note that Playa Vista HOAs and modern apartment complexes (like those at RUNWAY) often have strict non-smoking policies.

Consumption Guidance

Public consumption is prohibited near parks and business centers. To avoid heavy fines from local HOAs or property managers, we recommend legal consumption in private residences or licensed lounges.
