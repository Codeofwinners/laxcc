Discover Manhattan Beach: The Pearl of the South Bay

Manhattan Beach is one of the cleanest, most upscale coastal cities in Los Angeles. Known for its polished vibe, elite volleyball scene, and stunning ocean views, it offers a premium beach-town experience just minutes from LAX and SoFi Stadium.

Iconic Landmarks

Visit the 900-foot Manhattan Beach Pier at the end of MB Blvd for sweeping views and year-round fishing. Walk or bike The Strand, a world-famous path for exercise and people-watching along multi-million dollar beachfront homes.

Downtown Vibe

Downtown is the heartbeat of the city. Walkable and relaxed, it's packed with high-end boutiques, award-winning restaurants, and cozy cafés. It’s cleaner and more upscale than Venice, and significantly less touristy than Santa Monica.

MB Library

The **Manhattan Beach Library** is a modern architectural gem with stunning floor-to-ceiling ocean views. It's a perfect, quiet spot to recharge or enjoy the South Bay's coastal beauty from a different perspective.
