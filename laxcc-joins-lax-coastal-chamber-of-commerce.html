What Is the LAX Coastal Chamber of Commerce?

The LAX Coastal Chamber of Commerce is a local business network that supports business growth, community partnerships, educational programs, local outreach, and advocacy throughout the LAX Coastal region.

In simple terms, it brings local businesses together so they can better serve the people who live, work, travel, and shop in the area. For LAXCC, joining the Chamber is another step toward becoming more connected with the community we proudly serve every day.
