What's the strongest infused pre-roll in LA right now? +

What does "infused" actually mean in a preroll? +
Infused pre-rolls take ground flower and add concentrate — usually kief, hash, distillate, live resin, or rosin. That boost is what pushes total THC into the 30–45% range instead of the 18–25% you'd get from plain flower.
Live resin vs. distillate vs. hash — which infusion hits hardest? +
Distillate adds the highest raw THC numbers but loses terpenes. Live resin and hash keep the strain's flavor and effects more intact. For pure potency: distillate. For potency + flavor: live resin (Jeeter, Sluggers, Raw Garden) or rosin (Lift Tickets).
