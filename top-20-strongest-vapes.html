Buying Smart

What makes a vape "strong" — and how to pick yours

🧪

THC % is the headline number

Flower usually tests 20–30% THC. Vape oil is a concentrate, so 90%+ is the norm here — every product on this list clears that bar. Higher numbers mean a stronger hit from less vapor.

💎

Distillate vs. live resin

Kingpen, CIZI and dompen lean on refined distillate — extremely high THC with terpenes added for flavor. Rove's Melted Diamonds pair distillate-level potency with live-resin terpenes for a fuller, strain-true taste.

🎯

New or low tolerance? Start small

An all-in-one disposable lets you take one measured puff and wait — no rolling, no guesswork. A 0.5g device like the STIIIZY Liiil is an easy, pocket-sized way to ease in before committing to a full gram.

Cartridge & Device Guide

Know the format before you buy

The Top 20 spans several hardware types. Here is what each one means at the counter.

🔌

510-Thread Cartridge

The universal standard. Screws onto any 510 battery — most "Reload" carts on this list are 510.

✨

All-in-One (AIO)

Battery and oil in one sealed device. Charge it, vape it, recycle it — nothing to assemble.

🧩

Disposable

A pre-charged AIO meant to finish and toss — like the STIIIZY Liiil. The simplest grab-and-go option.

💎

Melted Diamond Live Resin

Live resin blended with THCA diamonds — maximum potency with the strain's real terpene flavor.

🛢️

Distillate

Highly refined THC oil with terpenes reintroduced. Consistent, clean and the highest-testing format.

🔋

Ready-To-Use vs. Reload

"Ready-To-Use" includes the battery; "Reload" is the cartridge alone for a battery you already own.
