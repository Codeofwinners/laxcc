Santa Monica FAQs

Answering your questions about our dispensary.

How close is your dispensary to LAX Airport? expand_more

We are conveniently located just minutes from LAX Airport, making us the perfect stop for travelers arriving in or departing from Los Angeles. Our proximity allows you to quickly pick up the best cannabis products California has to offer immediately after your flight or right before you take off.

Is the drive from Santa Monica worth it? expand_more

Yes, definitely. While there are dispensaries closer to Santa Monica, our customers find the short 15-minute drive south via Lincoln Blvd/CA-1 to be incredibly rewarding. You'll gain access to a larger vault of exclusive products, often at better prices, and experience a level of service and selection that is hard to match.

Can I order online for express pickup? expand_more

Absolutely! Skip the line by browsing our live menu online. Place your order for flower, vapes, or edibles comfortably from your home in Santa Monica, and we'll have it packed and ready for expedited pickup when you arrive.

Do you offer deals for first-time customers? expand_more

We love welcoming new faces from the Westside! We offer exclusive specials for first-time visitors. Check our "Deals" page or ask your budtender upon arrival to unlock savings on your first premium cannabis purchase.

What types of cannabis products do you carry? expand_more

Our menu features an extensive variety of premium cannabis products. We carry high-potency flower, flavorful vapes, delicious edibles, potent concentrates, and convenient pre-rolls. We curate our selection to ensure we have the perfect option for every preference.

Is there parking available? expand_more

Yes! Unlike many cramped spots in LA, we offer hassle-free parking right at our location. You won't have to circle the block or pay for a meter—just pull in, park securely, and head inside.

What do I need to bring to enter? expand_more

You must be 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, Passport, or State ID) to enter. We scan IDs at the door to ensure compliance with California state regulations.

Do you stock high-terpene exotic brands? expand_more

Yes, we specialize in connoisseur-grade cannabis. We stock top California brands known for high terpene percentages and robust flavor profiles. Whether you're hunting for Limonene-heavy Sativas or Myrcene-rich Indicas, our budtenders can guide you to the most aromatic strains on the market.

What payment methods do you accept? expand_more

We are a cash-only dispensary. For your convenience, we have ATMs available on-site so you can easily withdraw cash upon arrival.

How much cannabis can I buy? expand_more

Adult-use customers (21+) can purchase up to 1 ounce (28.5 grams) of flower and up to 8 grams of concentrate (including vapes/edibles) per day. Our staff keeps track to ensure your transaction stays within legal limits.

Do you have a rewards program? expand_more

Yes! Join our loyalty club to earn points on every purchase. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future visits. It's the best way to get even more value from your favorite products.

Can I consume cannabis on-site? expand_more

In accordance with California law, consumption of cannabis is not permitted on the dispensary premises or in public areas. Please enjoy your products responsibly in a private residence.
