Know Before You Go

The Rules That Actually Matter

Three things worth getting straight before you buy a ticket — especially if you're visiting from out of state.

Check the Age Limit, and Bring ID

California adult-use cannabis is limited to adults 21 and over, and most events on this list are 21+ — but not all of them are, so check the age limit on the listing itself rather than assuming. The Hashathon Cup, for instance, admits 18 and over. Bring a valid government-issued ID either way, and check each event's bag and device rules before you leave the house; Puffcon in particular has ticket tiers with device requirements.

"Cannabis Friendly" Isn't a Legal Status

Public consumption is generally prohibited in California. You can legally consume at a licensed lounge, on private property where it's permitted, or at a properly licensed temporary cannabis event — state-approved events lasting one to four days where licensed sales and consumption may occur. An event calling itself "cannabis friendly" does not automatically mean either is authorized.

Sort Your Ride Out First

Driving under the influence of cannabis is illegal, and open cannabis containers must be sealed or kept in the trunk while you're in the vehicle. Book the rideshare or pick the designated driver before the event, not after. See the state's what's legal guidance for the full rundown.

What we deliberately left off this list

Trade-only shows. Events like the Network Show are business trade shows for brands, retailers, buyers and licensed operators — not consumer festivals. Great if you work in the industry, a waste of a ticket if you don't.

Events with conflicting dates. Camp Kushstock currently has listings showing different dates and locations, including one with the venue still marked TBD. We'll add it once the organizer confirms a single date and place.

In-store retailer nights. A lot of what shows up in a search for "cannabis events LA" is a shop running a promotion — a brand rep at a table, a discount tied to a qualifying purchase, a lounge session for that store's customers. Useful if you're already shopping there, but it's a store promotion, not an event, so none of them are on this list.

Lounges listed as "events." A lounge is a venue, not an event. We only listed something when there's a specific name, date, time and activity attached to it — a generic lounge listing didn't make the cut.

Expired 2025 events. Several old Kushstock and WEEDCon pages still rank well in search. They've already happened. Always check the year on the listing before you buy.
