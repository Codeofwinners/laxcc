Cannabis tablets deliver the same cannabinoids as edibles — without the sugar, calories, or inconsistent dosing. Here's why patients and experienced users prefer them.

Precise Dosing

Pharmaceutical-grade manufacturing means every pill is exactly 25mg, 50mg, or 100mg. No splitting gummies, no guessing — just consistent milligrams every time.

Zero Sugar & Additives

Most THC pills contain only cannabis extract and a carrier oil. No corn syrup, no gelatin, no artificial colors. Ideal for diabetics and clean-eating consumers.

Long-Lasting Effects

Oral absorption through the digestive tract produces effects lasting 4–8 hours — significantly longer than vaping (1–3 hrs) or smoking (1–2 hrs).

Discreet & Portable

A pill bottle looks like any supplement. No smell, no preparation, no equipment needed. Take one anywhere, anytime, without drawing attention.
