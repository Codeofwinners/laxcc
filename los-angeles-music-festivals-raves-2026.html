The Music Festivals

The big destination festivals — stadium and beach-side events you build a day (or a weekend) around. Most are in or right next to the city, which makes them the easiest picks if you're flying into LAX.

Festival • EDM/Hip-Hop Aug 1–2, 2026

HARD Summer

● Hollywood Park, Inglewood 18+

LA's biggest summer crossover festival — bass, house, and rap on a stadium scale at the SoFi Stadium complex. Two days, multiple stages, and 2026 headliners including Zedd, Kali Uchis, DJ Snake and Mau P. It's the closest major festival to the airport, which makes it the natural kickoff to the season.

The Raves

The dedicated EDM events — mostly Insomniac productions out in the Inland Empire and Long Beach, built around huge stages, late hours and full sensory production. These are the all-night ones, and most are 18+. Plan your ride: they're 45 minutes to an hour east of central LA.

● Glen Helen, San Bernardino 18+ • Camping

Insomniac's longest-running festival — decades of underground credibility paired with full big-stage production. Camping is available, and it sits about 60 miles east of LA. A classic first rave for a lot of SoCal heads.

Festival & Rave Tips for Tourists and Locals

A few things that save money, time and headaches whether it's your first LA festival or your fifteenth:

  • Buy from the official site only. Every link in this guide goes straight to the event's own page. Skip resale and lookalike sites — they're where marked-up and counterfeit tickets live.
  • Know the age limit. Most Insomniac raves are 18+; in-city festivals vary. You must be 21+ to drink alcohol or buy cannabis anywhere in California.
  • Plan the drive. HARD Summer and Head in the Clouds are in the city. Nocturnal, Escape and Countdown are out in San Bernardino — carpool, rideshare, or look at official shuttles and camping.
  • Pack smart. Bring ID, a little cash, sunscreen and comfortable shoes. Check each event's official site for its bag policy and what you can carry in.
  • Hydrate and pace yourself. Long days, big crowds and SoCal heat add up. Free water refill stations are standard — use them.
  • Screenshot your tickets and map. Cell service gets crushed when 30,000 people show up. Save your QR code and set a meetup spot before you lose each other.

Cannabis & the Festival Scene

California is a legal state, but the events themselves are a different story. Like any major ticketed event, these festivals and raves are effectively cannabis-free zones — you can't bring it in, and public consumption is illegal in California anyway (no sidewalks, parking lots, or venue grounds). The legal way to enjoy it is before or after, at a private residence, a cannabis-friendly hotel or rental, or a licensed consumption lounge.

And don't fly with it — cannabis is still federally illegal, so leave it out of your bags at LAX. Buy what you'll use on your trip and enjoy it the legal way.

FAQ — LA Festivals & Raves in 2026

What's the next big music festival or rave in LA? +
HARD Summer on August 1–2, 2026 at Hollywood Park in Inglewood is the next major event — a two-day EDM and hip-hop festival right by SoFi Stadium and minutes from LAX. After that, Head in the Clouds at the Rose Bowl (Aug 8) and Nocturnal Wonderland (Sep 19–20) follow.
Which LA festivals are easiest to reach from the airport? +
HARD Summer (Hollywood Park, Inglewood) is the closest to LAX, with Head in the Clouds at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena and Dreamstate SoCal in Long Beach also relatively central. The Insomniac raves — Nocturnal Wonderland, Escape Halloween and Countdown NYE — are out in San Bernardino, roughly an hour east, so plan a ride or camping.
Are LA raves 18+ or 21+? +
Most Insomniac raves — Nocturnal Wonderland, Escape Halloween, Dreamstate SoCal and Countdown NYE — are 18+. You still must be 21 or older to drink alcohol or to buy cannabis anywhere in California. Always confirm the exact age policy on the event's official site, since some VIP areas are 21+ only.
Can I bring cannabis into a festival or rave? +
No. These events are effectively cannabis-free zones, and public consumption is illegal in California regardless. The legal way to enjoy it is before or after the event at a private residence, a cannabis-friendly hotel or rental, or a licensed consumption lounge — not on the venue grounds, in the parking lot, or in a vehicle.
Where should I buy tickets? +
Only from each event's official website — every event in this guide links straight to its own page. Buying through official channels protects you from counterfeit QR codes and heavily marked-up resale tickets, and it's the only way to be sure your wristband or pass is valid.
Are all of these 2026 dates confirmed? +
Most are. HARD Summer, Head in the Clouds, Ohana, Nocturnal Wonderland, Escape Halloween, Dreamstate SoCal and Countdown NYE all have confirmed dates.
