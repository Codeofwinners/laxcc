Massachusetts cannabis law · FAQ

Cannabis FAQ for Cannabis Dispensary Customers

Quick answers to the questions our Pittsfield customers ask most — what's legal, how much you can buy and carry, and how to stay compliant in Massachusetts.
Q Is recreational cannabis legal near Pittsfield, MA?
Yes. Cannabis is legal throughout Massachusetts — including Pittsfield — for adults 21 and over. You'll need a valid government-issued photo ID to enter and to purchase.
Q How old do I have to be, and what ID do I need?
You must be 21 or older. Accepted ID includes a state-issued driver's license with a photo, a U.S. identification card, a military ID, or an ID card issued by any Canadian province.
Q How much cannabis can I buy, carry, and keep in Massachusetts?
As of 2026, Massachusetts raised the adult possession limit from 1 ounce to 2 ounces. Here's what's allowed for adults 21+:
Situation Legal limit / rule
Carry in public Up to 2 ounces of marijuana (21+)
At home Up to 10 ounces in your primary residence, plus cannabis grown from your legal plants
Home storage More than 2 ounces at home should be locked up
Growing Up to 6 plants per adult, max 12 per household
Gifting Adults 21+ may give up to 2 ounces to another adult 21+ — no payment, no public advertising
In a vehicle No open container in the passenger area; keep it sealed, ideally in the trunk or a locked glovebox
Across state lines Illegal — even if the other state has legal cannabis
Q Where can I legally use cannabis?
Not in public. It's illegal to smoke, vape, or eat cannabis in public spaces, and you can't use it while driving. Keep product sealed in the vehicle, and never mail it or carry it across state lines.
Q What are the penalties for breaking these rules?
Public consumption is a civil penalty of up to $100. An open container in a vehicle can be up to $500. Giving cannabis to someone under 21 can carry a fine of up to $2,000, up to 1 year imprisonment, or both.
