Know before you go

Hilton LAX is 100% non-smoking

The Hilton Los Angeles Airport does not allow smoking in rooms — the property is 100% non-smoking and strictly enforces this policy. Smoking in a room (or near windows, bathrooms, or hallways) triggers a deep-cleaning fee of up to $250.

For cannabis specifically: California prohibits public consumption — and most hotels (Hilton included) prohibit it entirely on property, even in outdoor smoking areas. Per the California Department of Cannabis Control, consumption is limited to private residences or licensed lounges.

Best advice: Don't smoke at the Hilton. Edibles, tinctures, or transdermal patches are the smoke-free options that keep you compliant. If you want to consume, look at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge in Hawthorne or West Hollywood — staff at LAXCC can point you to one.
